Quick answer: Noorani Qaida is a beginner Arabic reading course that teaches the 28 Arabic letters, their correct pronunciation (makhraj), short and long vowels, and how letters join to form words. At Almuallim it is taught one-to-one online by qualified teachers, in UK time, and usually takes 4–8 months to complete depending on the student's pace.

What you'll learn in Noorani Qaida

Every lesson builds a small, solid step — no rushing, no skipping.

The 28 Arabic letters

Recognising every letter in its isolated form, and hearing the difference between similar-sounding letters.

Makhraj (correct pronunciation)

Where each letter is pronounced from — throat, tongue, lips — so recitation is accurate from day one.

Harakat (vowels)

Fatha, kasra, damma, sukoon and shadda — the short marks that change how a letter sounds.

Joining letters

How letters connect at the beginning, middle and end of a word — the bridge to reading real Arabic.

Madd (elongation)

Stretching vowel sounds for the correct number of counts — the first real Tajweed rule.

Reading practice words

Short Qur'anic words and phrases so students feel the progress before moving to full verses.

الْمَنْهَج

The Noorani Qaida curriculum

Taught in three stages, each with clear milestones.

  1. Stage 1 — Letters & sounds

    Learning each Arabic letter by shape and sound. Students practise hearing and repeating, and start writing letters to reinforce recognition.

  2. Stage 2 — Harakat & joining

    Short vowels, sukoon, shadda, and how letters join into two-, three- and four-letter words. This is where reading clicks for most students.

  3. Stage 3 — Madd & first verses

    Elongation rules and reading short Qur'anic words. By the end of this stage, students are ready to move into Qur'an Reading with Tajweed.

Who Noorani Qaida is for

✓ Children from age 5

Young beginners who cannot yet read Arabic letters. Lessons are short, patient and built around a child's attention span.

✓ Adults starting from scratch

Adults who never learned Arabic letters, or who learned long ago and want to restart properly.

✓ Reverts to Islam

New Muslims who want to read the Qur'an in Arabic — no prior Arabic knowledge needed.

✗ Not for fluent readers

If you can already read joined Arabic words, start with Qur'an Reading with Tajweed instead.

Noorani Qaida — common questions

What age can a child start Noorani Qaida?

Most children begin around age 5, once they can focus for a 20–30 minute lesson. Adults and older beginners are equally welcome.

How long does Noorani Qaida take to complete?

With two or three 1-to-1 lessons per week and short daily practice, most children complete Noorani Qaida in 4–8 months.

Do I need to buy a Noorani Qaida book?

No. Your teacher will share the Qaida pages on screen during class, and can send a PDF for home practice.

Is Noorani Qaida suitable for adults?

Yes. Adults who cannot yet read Arabic letters use the same syllabus, at an adult pace and with adult materials.
