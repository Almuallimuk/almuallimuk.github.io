3. Definitions

  • "Student" — the person attending the classes, whether a child or an adult.
  • "Parent" — the parent or legal guardian enrolling a student under 18.
  • "Teacher" — a qualified tutor engaged by Almuallim to deliver lessons.
  • "Class" — a live, one-to-one online lesson.
  • "Trial Class" — the first, free class offered to new students.
  • "Enrolment" — the point at which a student begins paid regular classes with us.

4. Eligibility & Enrolment

  • Students of all ages are welcome. Most children begin Noorani Qaida from around age 5.
  • Enquiries for students under 16 must be made by a parent or legal guardian.
  • Enrolment is confirmed once a schedule is agreed and, where applicable, the first payment is received.
  • We reserve the right to decline an enrolment if we are unable to meet a student's needs appropriately.

5. Free Trial Class

  • Every new student is entitled to one free trial class.
  • The trial class is used to assess the student's level, discuss goals, and confirm that the teacher is a good fit.
  • There is no obligation to enrol after the trial.
  • The trial is limited to one per student.

6. Classes & Scheduling

  • All classes are held online, one-to-one, via video call.
  • Sessions are scheduled in UK time and confirmed in advance with the parent or student.
  • Class length and frequency depend on the plan agreed at enrolment.
  • Students are expected to join on time. Lessons cannot be extended for late arrivals.
  • Occasional schedule adjustments can be made with reasonable notice (see Section 8).

7. Fees & Payment

  • Fees are agreed with you at enrolment and depend on the number of classes per week and the length of each session.
  • Payment is due in advance, in line with the agreed billing cycle (weekly or monthly).
  • Accepted payment methods will be confirmed by our team at enrolment.
  • We do not store card details on our website.
  • Fees may be adjusted in the future with reasonable advance notice. Any change will not affect classes already paid for.
No hidden charges: the amount we agree at enrolment is the amount you pay. If anything is unclear, please ask before enrolling.

8. Cancellations, Rescheduling & Missed Classes

By the student or parent

  • If you need to reschedule a class, please give us as much notice as possible — ideally at least 12 hours before the scheduled time.
  • Classes cancelled with reasonable notice will be rescheduled at no extra cost.
  • Classes missed without notice ("no-shows") may be counted as delivered and may not be refundable.

By Almuallim

  • If a teacher is unavailable, we will offer a replacement teacher or reschedule the class.
  • If we cannot deliver a class and cannot reschedule it, the equivalent amount will be credited or refunded.

Stopping classes

  • You may stop classes at any time by notifying us. There are no long-term contracts.
  • We ask for reasonable notice (ideally one week) so the teacher can be reassigned.

9. Refund Policy

  • Classes not yet delivered that have been paid for in advance are eligible for a refund on request.
  • Classes already delivered are non-refundable.
  • If you cancel a block of classes mid-cycle, we will refund the unused portion on a pro-rata basis.
  • Refunds are processed within 7–14 working days to the original payment method.
  • In the unlikely event that we are unable to provide a suitable teacher for your child, any undelivered fees will be refunded in full.
UK Consumer Rights: Nothing in this policy affects your statutory rights under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 or the Consumer Rights Act 2015.

10. Teacher Assignment & Changes

  • Every teacher is assessed for Tajweed accuracy and teaching ability before joining Almuallim.
  • We match students with teachers based on level, goals, availability and — where requested — gender preference.
  • If the assigned teacher is unavailable long-term, we will offer a suitable replacement.
  • You may request a teacher change if the fit isn't right. We will do our best to accommodate this.

11. Student & Parent Responsibilities

  • Provide a quiet, safe space for the class, with minimal distraction.
  • Ensure the student joins on time with the required materials (Qaida, Mushaf, notebook).
  • Encourage regular home practice — typically 15–30 minutes daily — as advised by the teacher.
  • Communicate promptly if the student is unwell, unable to attend, or has any special learning needs.
  • Keep contact details up to date so we can reach you about scheduling.

12. Code of Conduct

To keep classes beneficial and respectful for everyone, we ask that:

  • Students treat teachers with respect and courtesy at all times.
  • Parents do not interrupt lessons unless necessary.
  • No abusive, discriminatory or inappropriate behaviour is tolerated — towards teachers, staff, or other students.
  • Teachers maintain a professional, respectful and encouraging environment.
  • Any concern about a teacher's conduct should be reported to us immediately at info@almuallim.uk.

We reserve the right to suspend or end classes if this Code of Conduct is repeatedly breached.

13. Technology Requirements

  • Classes are delivered via video call. You will need a phone, tablet, or computer with a working camera and microphone.
  • A stable internet connection is essential.
  • No special software or equipment is required — we will confirm the platform before your first class.
  • If technical issues on your side cause a class to be cut short, we will try to reschedule. If issues are on our side, we will always reschedule or credit the class.
