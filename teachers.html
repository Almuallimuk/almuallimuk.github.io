How we verify our teachers

We don't just say our teachers are qualified — we check, and we tell you exactly what we check.

Formal certificates reviewed

Every teacher provides original certificates from recognised Islamic education boards, such as Tanzeem-ul-Madaris Ahl-e-Sunnat Pakistan.

Tajweed accuracy assessed

Each candidate recites live and is assessed on Tajweed rules, Makharij accuracy, and Qirat fluency before being approved to teach.

Teaching ability tested

Knowledge alone doesn't make a teacher. We test how clearly a candidate explains a concept, corrects a mistake, and keeps a student motivated.

English-medium instruction

Our teachers are experienced with UK students whose first language is English. Concepts are explained in plain English — not just Arabic terminology.

Parent feedback reviewed

After each month, we review parent feedback on every teacher. If a teacher consistently fails to meet our standard, they no longer teach at Almuallim.

Female tutors available

For sisters and young girls, we provide dedicated female tutors. Female-only classes are taught by Muallima Rabia Batool and other qualified female teachers, at no extra cost.

Recognised Institution

Tanzeem-ul-Madaris Ahl-e-Sunnat Pakistan

Our teachers' primary qualifications — including Dars-e-Nizami (Aalmia), Qiraat, Qirat and Hafiz Sanads — are issued by Tanzeem-ul-Madaris Ahl-e-Sunnat Pakistan, a recognised Islamic education board with a national network of affiliated madaris.

How our teachers actually teach

Every lesson follows the same structured approach — the same for a 5-year-old starting Qaida and an adult returning to Qur'an reading.

  1. Free trial & level assessment

    Your child's first lesson is a free trial. The teacher assesses current reading level, pronunciation accuracy, and any Tajweed gaps.

  2. Personalised lesson plan

    A plan is built around the student's level and pace — not a generic syllabus. Reviewed and adjusted as the student progresses.

  3. Explain → demonstrate → practise

    Teachers use PowerPoint for Tajweed concepts, then the student practises live during the Zoom lesson. Correction happens in real time.

  4. Guided home practice

    Between lessons, students receive short, clear practice tasks — typically 15–30 minutes of daily revision.

  5. Progress reports to parents

    Parents receive regular feedback on what has been covered, what needs revision, and what comes next.

  6. Milestone celebration

    Completing a Qaida level, a Juz, or a full Qur'an review is marked and celebrated. Motivation matters at every age.

Common questions about our teachers

Are Almuallim teachers formally qualified?

Yes. Muallima Rabia Batool holds a Dars-e-Nizami (Aalmia) qualification after 6 years of formal study, plus a Qiraat & Naat certification. Hafiz Qari Faizan holds a Hafiz-ul-Quran Sanad and Qirat Sanad. Qari Haroon Bastami holds a Qirat Sanad and Dars-e-Nizami studies. All from Tanzeem-ul-Madaris Ahl-e-Sunnat Pakistan.

Can I choose a female or male teacher?

Yes. Female students and young boys (up to age 10) are taught by Muallima Rabia Batool. Male students and older boys are taught by Hafiz Qari Faizan and Qari Haroon Bastami.

Who teaches Hifz at Almuallim?

Hifz programmes are taught by Hafiz Qari Faizan, a Hafiz-ul-Quran with a formal Hafiz Sanad and years of experience guiding students through complete Qur'an memorisation.

Who teaches English Tarjuma and Islamiat?

Qari Haroon Bastami specialises in English Tarjuma (translation) of the Qur'an and Islamiat for UK students, alongside Tajweed and Qirat.

How do you verify teacher credentials?

Every teacher provides original certificates from recognised institutions such as Tanzeem-ul-Madaris Ahl-e-Sunnat Pakistan. We verify Tajweed accuracy, teaching ability, and English communication skills.

Do you have other teachers besides those listed?

Yes. The teachers listed are our core team. We also work with additional qualified male and female teachers who are matched to students based on subject, level and schedule.

What language do your teachers use?

All lessons are delivered in English-medium, making Tajweed rules, Makharij, Tarjuma and Islamic Studies concepts easy to follow for UK students.

Can I request the same teacher every week?

Yes. Students keep the same teacher for consistency, unless you request a change. We do not rotate teachers between lessons.
