You don't need to know Arabic to start

The most common worry we hear from reverts is: "I can't read Arabic — can I still learn the Quran?"

Yes. Reading the Quran and speaking Arabic are two separate skills. Millions of Muslims around the world recite the Quran accurately without being fluent in Arabic. What you need is not Arabic fluency — it is letter recognition, correct pronunciation, and consistent practice. That is exactly what our beginner classes are built around.

  • Recitation (Tajweed)Learning to read the Arabic script of the Quran with correct pronunciation — this is what most reverts start with.
  • Understanding (Tafseer)Learning what the verses mean through English translation and explanation — this grows alongside your recitation.
  • Arabic as a languageLearning to speak, write, and converse in Arabic — a separate, longer-term study that some students choose later.
  • Taught entirely in EnglishYour teacher explains every letter, sound, and rule in clear English. You are not expected to understand Arabic instructions.

الْقَاعِدَةُ النُّورَانِيَّة

Learning the Arabic letters (Noorani Qaida)

Noorani Qaida is the foundation book used worldwide to teach Quran reading. It is not just for children — it is the standard starting point for adults too.

What Noorani Qaida covers

Noorani Qaida stages
StageWhat you learnTypical duration
LettersShapes, names, and correct pronunciation of all 28 Arabic letters2–4 weeks
HarakatFatha, Kasra, Damma — short vowels that change letter sounds2–3 weeks
JoiningHow letters connect to form words3–4 weeks
Sukoon & ShaddahReading without vowels and doubled letters3–4 weeks
MaddElongation rules — stretching sounds for the correct length2–3 weeks
Short SurahsApplying everything to read short verses from Juz Amma4–6 weeks

Adults often progress faster than children in Qaida because they can focus for longer, understand phonetic explanations, and stay motivated by a clear goal. Many adult beginners complete Qaida within 2–4 months with 15–30 minutes of daily practice.

Frequently asked questions

Can I learn to read the Quran if I can't read Arabic at all?

Yes. Most students who start with Almuallim have no prior Arabic knowledge. You begin with Noorani Qaida, which teaches the Arabic alphabet, letter sounds, and how letters join — step by step, at your pace. Reading the Quran does not require you to speak Arabic as a language.

Do I need to learn Arabic as a language before learning to read the Quran?

No. Reading the Quran (recitation with Tajweed) and understanding Arabic as a language are two separate skills. You can learn to recite accurately without being fluent in Arabic. Understanding the meaning comes through translation and Tafseer, which Almuallim teaches alongside recitation.

Should a new Muslim start with Noorani Qaida?

For most adult beginners, yes. Noorani Qaida is the standard foundation — it builds letter recognition, correct pronunciation (Makharij), and joining rules before you approach full verses. Adults often progress faster than children because they can focus and understand explanations.

Can adults learn to read the Quran from scratch?

Absolutely. Many Almuallim students are adults in their 30s, 40s, and 50s starting from zero. One-to-one lessons mean no group pressure and no comparison — your teacher adapts entirely to your pace and goals.

Can I request a female Quran teacher?

Yes. Female tutors are available for sisters and young girls who prefer a female-only learning environment. Request a female teacher when booking your free trial class.

What happens in the first lesson?

Your first lesson is a free trial. The teacher has an informal conversation about your background, goals, and prior experience, then begins with the very first steps — typically Arabic letters. No test, no pressure, no obligation to continue.

How long does it take to learn to read the Quran?

With one-to-one lessons and 15–30 minutes of daily practice, most adult beginners complete Noorani Qaida and reach basic Quran reading fluency within 6–12 months. Progress depends on consistency, not age or prior ability.

Can I learn the Quran and understand its meaning at the same time?

Yes. Almuallim teaches recitation with Tajweed alongside English translation and Tafseer. As reading improves, your teacher introduces the meanings of the verses you are reciting, so recitation and understanding grow together.

Are online Quran lessons suitable for complete beginners?

Yes. Online lessons work particularly well for adult beginners — privacy, flexibility, and one-to-one attention. All you need is a phone, tablet, or laptop with a camera and a stable internet connection.

Can I learn privately without joining a group class?

Yes. Every Almuallim lesson is one-to-one. No group classes, no shared sessions, no other students listening. Your lesson is entirely private between you and your teacher.
