Why our pricing is structured this way

We keep pricing simple and honest — so you always know exactly what you're paying for.

Lower rate at higher frequency

Classes cost £4.00 each on Starter, £3.50 on Standard, and £3.00 on Intensive. Regular lessons deliver faster progress and better retention — so students who study more pay less per session.

30-minute classes, every package

We deliberately cap every lesson at 30 minutes. For young learners especially, this keeps focus high and makes daily home practice easier to sustain. Longer isn't always better.

Same price for every subject

Noorani Qaida, Tajweed, Hifz, Tarjuma and Islamiat all cost the same. You don't pay more for advanced courses, and you can switch subjects without a fee change.

No contract, no hidden fees

No registration fee, no admission fee, no automatic renewal. Pay monthly, pause any time, cancel any time. We only want families to stay because they're happy — not because they're locked in.

Pricing questions

How much do online Quran classes cost in the UK?

Plans start from £32 per month for 2 classes per week (£4 per 30-minute class). The Standard package is £42 per month for 3 classes per week (£3.50 per class), and the Intensive package is £60 per month for 5 classes per week (£3 per class). Every new student gets a free trial class first.

Is there a registration or admission fee?

No. There is no registration fee, admission fee or any hidden charge. You pay only the monthly package amount, and the free trial class is genuinely free with no card required.

Do you offer sibling discounts?

Yes. When two or more children from the same family enrol, each additional child receives a 10% discount on their monthly package. The discount applies for as long as both children remain enrolled.

Is there a loyalty discount for prepaying?

Yes. Prepay 3 months upfront and receive 1 free class added to your plan. Prepay 6 months upfront and receive 3 free classes added. This rewards families who commit to steady, long-term progress.

How long is each class?

Every class is 30 minutes, one-to-one, live over Zoom. We deliberately cap lessons at 30 minutes to keep young learners focused and to make home practice easier to sustain.

Can I pause or cancel my plan?

Yes. There are no long-term contracts. You can pause or cancel at any time — just let us know before the next billing date. Many families pause during Ramadan travel or school holidays and resume afterwards.

Is the same price for all subjects?

Yes. Whether the student is learning Noorani Qaida, Tajweed, Hifz, Tarjuma or Islamiat, the monthly package price is the same. You do not pay extra for advanced courses.

What if I miss a class?

If you give us at least 4 hours notice, we will reschedule the class at no extra cost. Classes missed without notice are counted as used. Teacher cancellations are always rescheduled or credited.
