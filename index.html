A learning path, from the first letter to Hifz

Courses are sequenced so every student — child or adult, complete beginner or advanced reader — has a clear next step.

01

Noorani Qaida

The starting point for absolute beginners: Arabic letters, correct pronunciation (makhraj) and joining letters into words, before moving into the Qur'an itself.

Ages 5+ · Beginner
02

Qur'an Reading with Tajweed

Step-by-step recitation practice with the rules of Tajweed — correct articulation, elongation and pausing — so the Qur'an is read as it was revealed.

Kids & Adults · Beginner–Intermediate
03

Hifz-ul-Qur'an

Structured memorisation with daily revision (murajaʿah), designed around each student's pace, school schedule and long-term retention.

Committed Students · Intermediate–Advanced
04

Tafseer & Islamic Studies

Understanding the meaning and context behind the verses, alongside essential Islamic manners, duas and seerah for a rounded Islamic education.

All Ages · Any Level

The virtue of learning and reciting the Qur'an

A few verses that speak directly to why we recite with Tajweed, and why learning the Qur'an holds such a high place.

وَرَتِّلِ الْقُرْآنَ تَرْتِيلًا

"…and recite the Qur'an slowly and distinctly, giving each letter its due."

Surah Al-Muzzammil 73:4

وَلَقَدْ يَسَّرْنَا الْقُرْآنَ لِلذِّكْرِ فَهَلْ مِنْ مُدَّكِرٍ

"We have made the Qur'an easy to remember and take heed from — so is there anyone who will reflect?"

Surah Al-Qamar 54:17

الَّذِينَ آتَيْنَاهُمُ الْكِتَابَ يَتْلُونَهُ حَقَّ تِلَاوَتِهِ

"Those to whom We gave the Book recite it as it truly deserves to be recited."

Surah Al-Baqarah 2:121

خَيْرُكُمْ مَنْ تَعَلَّمَ الْقُرْآنَ وَعَلَّمَهُ

"The best among you is the one who learns the Qur'an and teaches it to others."

Hadith — Sahih al-Bukhari

Common questions from UK parents

Do you teach students across the whole UK?

Yes. Because classes are online, we teach students in London, Birmingham, Manchester, Leeds, Glasgow, Liverpool, Sheffield, Bristol, Edinburgh, Cardiff and every other UK city — all in UK time.

Is there a free trial class?

Yes, every new student gets a free trial class so you can meet the teacher and see how the classes work before enrolling.

What age can my child start?

Most children begin Noorani Qaida from around age 5, but we also welcome older beginners and adults at any age.

Can I request a female teacher?

Yes, female tutors are available for sisters and young girls who prefer a female-only learning environment.

What do I need for an online class?

Just a phone, tablet or laptop with a camera and a stable internet connection — no special software or equipment required.
