What is Almuallim Online Quran Academy?

A UK-focused online Quran academy built around one-to-one teaching, not group classes.

Almuallim is a one-to-one online Quran academy serving students across the UK. Rather than squeezing children into fixed group schedules, every lesson is built around the individual — their level, their pace, their goals. That means a 5-year-old starting Noorani Qaida has a different experience to an adult returning to Quran reading, even though both are taught by the same standard of qualified, vetted teacher.

  • Qualified, vetted teachersEvery tutor is assessed for Tajweed accuracy and teaching ability before joining.
  • Structured curriculumNoorani Qaida through to Hifz, with clear milestones at every stage.
  • One-to-one online classesEvery lesson is private — no group pacing, no waiting, no falling behind.
  • Sisters-only optionFemale tutors available for sisters and young girls who prefer a female-only learning environment.

Listening before reciting
Every Almuallim lesson includes guided listening — the teacher recites the passage, the student repeats it, and corrections happen in real time. Between lessons, students are encouraged to listen to the week's passage before practising aloud, so the ear learns the sound before the tongue has to reproduce it.

Almuallim began with a simple frustration: UK Muslim families kept telling us the same thing — Quran classes that fit real life were almost impossible to find. Common questions from UK parents

Do you teach students across the whole UK?

Yes. Because classes are online, we teach students in London, Birmingham, Manchester, Leeds, Glasgow, Liverpool, Sheffield, Bristol, Edinburgh, Cardiff and every other UK city — all in UK time.

Is there a free trial class?

Yes, every new student gets a free trial class so you can meet the teacher and see how the classes work before enrolling.

What age can my child start?

Most children begin Noorani Qaida from around age 5, but we also welcome older beginners and adults at any age.

Can I request a female teacher?

Yes, female tutors are available for sisters and young girls who prefer a female-only learning environment.

What do I need for an online class?

Just a phone, tablet or laptop with a camera and a stable internet connection — no special software or equipment required.

How much do online Quran classes cost in the UK?

Our fees are competitive with other UK online Quran academies and depend on the number of classes per week and session length. Contact us for a clear breakdown with no hidden charges.

How long does it take to learn to read the Quran with Tajweed?

With weekly one-to-one lessons and 15–30 minutes of daily home practice, most children reach basic Quran reading fluency within 6–12 months. Tajweed mastery develops progressively.
