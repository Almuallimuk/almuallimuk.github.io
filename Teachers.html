Common questions about our teachers

Who are the teachers at Almuallim?

Our team includes Ustadha Rabia Batool (Head Teacher), Hafiz Qari Faizan, and Qari Haroon Bastami. All teachers are qualified from Tanzeem-ul-Madaris Ahl-e-Sunnat Pakistan and have extensive experience in teaching Quran online.

Can I choose a male or female teacher?

Yes. We offer both male and female teachers. Ustadha Rabia Batool teaches female students and young boys (up to age 10), while Hafiz Qari Faizan and Qari Haroon Bastami teach male students and older boys.

Are the teachers qualified to teach Tajweed and Hifz?

Absolutely. All our teachers hold formal qualifications in Dars-e-Nizami, Qirat, and Tajweed. They have years of experience in teaching Hifz, Noorani Qaida, and advanced Tajweed rules.

What language do the teachers use?

Our teachers are experienced in English-medium instruction, making it easy for UK students to understand Tajweed rules, Makharij, and Islamic Studies concepts clearly in English.
