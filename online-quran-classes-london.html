Why online Quran classes work for London families

London has one of the largest Muslim populations in the Western world — over 1.3 million Muslims across 32 boroughs. But a long commute, crowded Tube lines and packed local madrasah timetables make weekly in-person Quran classes a real challenge.

According to the 2021 Census, around 15% of London's population identifies as Muslim, with some East London boroughs close to 40%. That density is wonderful for the community — but it also means local masjids and madrasahs fill up fast, and group evening classes often move too quickly for some children and too slowly for others.

Common questions from London parents

Do you offer online Quran classes across all London boroughs?

Yes. Almuallim teaches students in all 32 London boroughs — including Tower Hamlets, Newham, Redbridge, Waltham Forest, Brent, Croydon, Hounslow, Ealing, Hackney, Haringey, Barnet and Barking & Dagenham — all scheduled in UK time.

How much do online Quran classes cost in London?

Plans start from £32 per month for 2 classes per week (£4 per 30-minute class). The Standard package is £42 per month for 3 classes per week (£3.50 per class), and the Intensive package is £60 per month for 5 classes per week (£3 per class). Sibling and loyalty discounts are available. See full pricing →

Do you offer sibling discounts?

Yes. When two or more children from the same family enrol, each additional child receives a 10% discount on their monthly package. The discount applies for as long as both children remain enrolled.

Is there a loyalty discount for paying upfront?

Yes. Prepay 3 months upfront and receive 1 free class added to your plan. Prepay 6 months upfront and receive 3 free classes added.

How do online classes fit around a London commute?

Lessons are 1-to-1 over Zoom with flexible UK-time slots — early morning before commute, after-school, after-Maghrib, or evening after work. Because there is no travel, a 30-minute lesson is genuinely 30 minutes.

Is the first Quran class in London free?

Yes, every new London student gets a completely free trial class with no card details required, so you can meet the teacher and see how the classes work before enrolling.

Can I get a female Quran teacher in London?

Yes. Muallima Rabia Batool (Head Teacher) teaches female students and young boys, and additional qualified female tutors are available for sisters and young girls across London — at no extra cost.

Can adults in London learn Quran online?

Yes. Many of our London students are adults returning to Qur'an reading or starting from scratch. Lessons are flexible — early morning before commute, evening after work, or weekend.

Do you teach English Tarjuma and Islamiat in London?

Yes. Qari Haroon Bastami specialises in English Tarjuma (Qur'an translation) and Islamiat for UK students, alongside Tajweed and Qirat.

What do I need for an online Quran class in London?

Just a phone, tablet or laptop with a camera and a stable internet connection — no special software or equipment required.
