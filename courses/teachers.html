What we look for in a teacher

Not every qualified reciter can teach. Our standard is both — Tajweed accuracy and the ability to make a student progress.

Ijazah or equivalent

Every teacher holds a formal qualification in Qur'an recitation with Tajweed, verified before joining.

Assessed teaching ability

We test every candidate's ability to explain, correct and encourage — not just recite.

Experienced with UK students

Our teachers are experienced with children and adults whose first language is English.

Patient with beginners

Especially important for young children and adult beginners returning to reading after years.

Reliable scheduling

Our teachers keep their slots. If a teacher can't make a lesson, we reschedule it — not lose it.

Ongoing review

Parent feedback is reviewed regularly, and teachers who don't meet our standard are removed.

كَيْفَ يُدَرِّسُون

How our teachers work with your child

A consistent, structured approach — every lesson, every teacher.

  1. Assess on day one

    Your child's first free trial lesson includes an informal assessment. The teacher maps their current level and discusses goals with you.

  2. Build a plan around them

    Lessons are tailored — not a generic syllabus. A 5-year-old starting Qaida has a different plan to an adult returning to reading.

  3. Teach, listen, correct

    Every lesson includes guided listening, in-class recitation, and immediate correction. Students hear it, then repeat it, then read it.

  4. Set home practice

    Short, clear tasks between lessons — typically 15–30 minutes daily — so progress stays steady between sessions.

  5. Report progress

    Parents receive regular feedback: what's been covered, what needs revision, and what comes next.

  6. Celebrate milestones

    Completing a Qaida level, a Juz, or a full Qur'an review is marked. Motivation matters at every age.

Questions about our teachers

Are your teachers qualified?

Yes. Every Almuallim teacher holds an ijazah or equivalent qualification in Qur'an recitation with Tajweed, and is assessed for teaching ability before joining.

Can I request a female teacher?

Yes. Female tutors are available for sisters and young girls who prefer a female-only learning environment. There is no extra cost.

Will my child have the same teacher every lesson?

Yes. Students keep the same teacher for consistency, unless you request a change. We do not rotate teachers between lessons.

What languages do your teachers speak?

Our teachers speak English, Urdu and Arabic, and are experienced teaching students whose first language is English.

What if my child doesn't get on with their teacher?

Tell us. We will match you with a different teacher, no questions asked. The trial class exists exactly so you can check the fit before committing.
