Quick answer: Sisters-Only Classes at Almuallim are one-to-one online Quran lessons taught exclusively by qualified female tutors. They cover Noorani Qaida, Qur'an Reading with Tajweed, Hifz-ul-Qur'an and Islamic Studies, and are available to girls and women of all ages across the UK. Why a sisters-only environment?

For many families, a female teacher is not a preference — it is a requirement. We make it the default, not a special request.

Comfort from the first lesson

Girls and women often engage more freely with a female teacher, especially in the early stages of learning.

Qualified female Huffaz & Qaris

Our female tutors are assessed for Tajweed accuracy and teaching ability before joining — the same standard as all our teachers.

All courses available

Sisters-Only Classes cover every course: Qaida, Tajweed, Hifz and Islamic Studies — not just beginner reading.

No extra cost

Requesting a female tutor does not add to the fee. It is part of the standard service.

UK-time scheduling

Lessons are scheduled in UK time, with after-school and evening slots available.

Parent visibility

Parents receive regular progress updates, so you always know how the student is advancing.

Courses available with female tutors

Every Almuallim course can be taught by a female tutor.

  1. Noorani Qaida

    Beginner Arabic reading — letters, makhraj and joining words — taught patiently by a female tutor.

  2. Qur'an Reading with Tajweed

    Correct recitation with all the Tajweed rules, at a pace built around the student.

  3. Hifz-ul-Qur'an

    Structured memorisation with daily muraja'ah, taught by qualified female Huffaz.

  4. Tafseer & Islamic Studies

    Qur'anic meaning, seerah, duas and Islamic manners — at an age-appropriate level.

Sisters-Only Classes — common questions

Are all Sisters-Only teachers female?

Yes. Every teacher in the Sisters-Only programme is a qualified female tutor, vetted for Tajweed accuracy and teaching ability.

What ages can join Sisters-Only Classes?

Girls and women of all ages — typically from age 5 upwards. Young girls and adult sisters are both welcome.

Can sisters study Hifz with a female teacher?

Yes. Sisters-Only Classes cover Noorani Qaida, Tajweed reading, Hifz-ul-Qur'an and Islamic Studies, all taught by female tutors.

Is there an extra cost for Sisters-Only Classes?

No. Sisters-Only Classes are priced the same as our standard one-to-one classes. There is no premium for requesting a female tutor.
