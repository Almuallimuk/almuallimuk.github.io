Quick answer: Tafseer & Islamic Studies at Almuallim teaches the meaning and context of Qur'anic verses alongside seerah (the life of the Prophet ﷺ), essential duas, and Islamic manners. It is taught one-to-one online to UK students of all ages, and can be studied alongside Tajweed or Hifz.

What you'll learn

Four strands, taught at an age-appropriate level.

Tafseer of key surahs

Verse-by-verse study of Surah Al-Fatiha, the short surahs, and selected passages — meaning, context and lessons.

Seerah (Prophetic biography)

The life of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ — his character, key events, and the lessons they hold for daily life.

Duas & adhkar

Essential daily supplications — morning, evening, before eating, travelling — with their meanings.

Islamic manners (adab)

How to behave with parents, neighbours, friends, and in everyday situations — grounded in Qur'an and Sunnah.

Pillars & basics of faith

The five pillars, articles of faith, and the basics every Muslim should know — explained simply.

Stories of the prophets

Stories of earlier prophets — Adam, Nuh, Ibrahim, Musa, Isa (peace be upon them) — with their timeless lessons.

الْمَنْهَج

The Islamic Studies curriculum

Structured by age and prior knowledge.

  1. Stage 1 — Foundations (ages 5–8)

    Short duas, basic manners, simple stories of the prophets, and the five pillars explained at a child's level.

  2. Stage 2 — Building knowledge (ages 9–12)

    Seerah in detail, Tafseer of the short surahs, and a wider set of daily duas and adhkar.

  3. Stage 3 — Deeper study (teens)

    Tafseer of longer surahs, thematic study of the Qur'an, and discussion of contemporary issues in light of Islamic principles.

  4. Stage 4 — Adult study

    Adults can begin at any stage, focusing on Tafseer, seerah or practical Islamic living, depending on their goals.

Islamic Studies — common questions

What is the difference between Tafseer and Islamic Studies?

Tafseer focuses on understanding the Qur'an verse by verse. Islamic Studies is broader: seerah, duas, manners and basics of faith. Almuallim combines both in one course.

Can my child take Tafseer without finishing Tajweed?

Yes. Tafseer focuses on meaning rather than recitation, so it can be studied at any reading level — many students take it alongside Tajweed.

What age is Islamic Studies suitable for?

All ages, with age-appropriate materials. Younger children focus on duas, manners and stories; older students cover seerah and Tafseer in depth.

Do you teach Tafseer in English or Arabic?

Both. Tafseer is taught in English for UK students, with Arabic verses read and explained. Arabic-medium instruction is available on request.
