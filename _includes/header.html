وَرَتِّلِ الْقُرْآنَ تَرْتِيلًا ◆ "And recite the Qur'an with measured recitation." — Al-Muzzammil 73:4 خَيْرُكُمْ مَنْ تَعَلَّمَ الْقُرْآنَ وَعَلَّمَهُ ◆ "The best among you are those who learn the Qur'an and teach it." — Sahih al-Bukhari 5027 وَلَقَدْ يَسَّرْنَا الْقُرْآنَ لِلذِّكْرِ فَهَلْ مِنْ مُدَّكِرٍ ◆ "And We have certainly made the Qur'an easy for remembrance — so is there any who will remember?" — Al-Qamar 54:17 اقْرَأْ بِاسْمِ رَبِّكَ الَّذِي خَلَقَ ◆ "Read in the name of your Lord who created." — Al-Alaq 96:1 يَرْفَعِ اللَّهُ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا مِنكُمْ وَالَّذِينَ أُوتُوا الْعِلْمَ دَرَجَاتٍ ◆ "Allah will raise those who have believed among you and those who were given knowledge, by degrees." — Al-Mujadila 58:11 الَّذِينَ آتَيْنَاهُمُ الْكِتَابَ يَتْلُونَهُ حَقَّ تِلَاوَتِهِ ◆ "Those to whom We gave the Book recite it as it truly deserves to be recited." — Al-Baqarah 2:121 إِنَّا نَحْنُ نَزَّلْنَا الذِّكْرَ وَإِنَّا لَهُ لَحَافِظُونَ ◆ "Indeed, it is We who sent down the Qur'an — and indeed, We will be its guardian." — Al-Hijr 15:9 وَقُل رَّبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا ◆ "And say: My Lord, increase me in knowledge." — Ta-Ha 20:114
