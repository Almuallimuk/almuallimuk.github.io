Why online Quran classes work for Birmingham families

Birmingham has one of the largest and fastest-growing Muslim populations of any city in the UK — which brings real pressure on local madrasah places.

According to the 2021 Census, close to 30% of Birmingham's population identifies as Muslim, and in wards such as Small Heath and Alum Rock that figure rises well above 80%. That density is a real strength for the community, but it also means many local madrasahs and after-Maghrib classes are running at capacity, with long waiting lists for a one-to-one slot. Online classes solve that bottleneck directly: no waiting list, no travel across the city in evening traffic, and a private lesson built around your child's pace rather than a fixed group timetable.

Who teaches at Almuallim

Every lesson is delivered live by a qualified teacher — never a pre-recorded video and never a group call.

Almuallim was built around a simple idea: a student who gets the teacher's full attention learns faster and stays motivated. That is why every class is one-to-one, every teacher is formally certified in Dars-e-Nizami, and every lesson is tailored to the individual student rather than a fixed group timetable.

Families across Birmingham choose Almuallim because they get a guaranteed slot, a teacher who knows their child by name, and clear feedback after every stage of progress.

Common questions from Birmingham parents

Do you offer online Quran classes in Birmingham?

Yes. Almuallim provides live one-to-one online Quran classes to students across Birmingham — including Small Heath, Sparkbrook, Alum Rock, Sparkhill, Bordesley Green, Handsworth, Aston, Perry Barr, Edgbaston and Moseley — all scheduled in UK time.

Why choose online Quran classes over a local Birmingham madrasah?

Birmingham is home to one of the largest Muslim communities in the UK, so many local madrasahs and evening classes are oversubscribed with long waiting lists. Online one-to-one classes give your child a guaranteed teacher, a private lesson pace, and a slot that fits your family's evening rather than a fixed group timetable.

How much do online Quran classes cost in Birmingham?

Plans start from £32 per month for 2 classes per week (£4 per 30-minute class). The Standard package is £42 per month for 3 classes per week (£3.50 per class), and the Intensive package is £60 per month for 5 classes per week (£3 per class). Sibling and loyalty discounts are available. See full pricing →

Do you offer sibling discounts?

Yes. When two or more children from the same family enrol, each additional child receives a 10% discount on their monthly package. The discount applies for as long as both children remain enrolled.

Is the first Quran class in Birmingham free?

Yes, every new Birmingham student gets a completely free trial class with no card details required, so you can meet the teacher and see how the classes work before enrolling.

Can I get a female Quran teacher in Birmingham?

Yes. Muallima Rabia Batool (Head Teacher) teaches female students and young boys, and additional qualified female tutors are available for sisters and young girls in Birmingham — at no extra cost.

Do you offer evening classes for working parents in Birmingham?

Yes. Because classes are online, there is no travel time across Birmingham after work or school, so we can offer tighter after-Maghrib and evening slots than a physical madrasah usually can.

Do you teach English Tarjuma and Islamiat in Birmingham?

Yes. Qari Haroon Bastami specialises in English Tarjuma (Qur'an translation) and Islamiat for UK students, alongside Tajweed and Qirat. This is popular with Birmingham parents who want their children to understand what they recite.

What do I need for an online Quran class in Birmingham?

Just a phone, tablet or laptop with a camera and a stable internet connection — no special software or equipment required.
